## Full description (1103/4000)

Fumipoka — your quiet offline companion.

Keep project notes, missions and Save Points together. Record what you finished, where you stopped, blockers and the next small action so it is easier to return. Rescue Mode helps collect a practical restart plan. Use focus sessions and project widgets alongside your private journal.

Choose light, dark, system or OLED appearance, local color palettes and a custom accent. Japanese-designed fonts and original café artwork bring warmth to everyday use.

Free forever. No ads, subscriptions, paid features, mandatory accounts, AI or external analytics. Core features work offline and personal records remain in private on-device storage. Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled.

You control exports through the Android document picker. Exported files are unencrypted, and a provider you select may store or sync them in the cloud. Keep private backups before changing devices.

Android permissions, battery settings and background limits can delay reminders.
