Network and third parties

This build has no Internet permission, advertising, tracking SDK, hosted analytics, or cloud synchronization. It does not upload your work to us. Opening a saved web link hands it to your browser, whose own privacy policy applies.

Backups you control

Export creates an unencrypted JSON document containing your app data and preferences at the location you choose. Anyone with access to it can read it. Your chosen document provider may upload or sync it under its own policy. Restore reads the file you select, previews its contents, and replaces local data only after confirmation.

Delete or keep a copy

Use Settings to export a copy or delete all local records and reset preferences. Uninstalling removes app storage. Exported documents are separate: delete them through their document provider. Android automatic backup is disabled. Deletion is logical removal, not a promise of forensic erasure from device storage.
